Balance routine enemy damage separately from boss and mini-boss encounters.

**Why:** The user reported players dying too easily to normal enemies and requested a modest base-damage reduction. Bosses have separately requested, deliberately tuned encounters.

**How to apply:** Include ordinary ranged attacks as well as contact damage when adjusting normal-enemy survivability. Do not compensate by raising enemy health or reducing rewards unless requested.
